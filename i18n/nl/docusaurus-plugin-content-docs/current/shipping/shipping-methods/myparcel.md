# MyParcel Overview

[**MyParcel**](https://www.myparcel.nl/) is an all-in-one shipping platform designed for online entrepreneurs, providing a quick and straightforward way to create shipping labels for various types of shipments. Whether it's parcels, letterbox parcels, post, or pallets, MyParcel offers a streamlined process to get your items ready for shipping across the world. They collaborate with top carriers like PostNL and DHL to ensure your shipments reach their destinations efficiently.

With MyParcel, you can process all your orders and generate shipping labels for parcels, letterbox parcels, post, or pallets with ease, create branded Track & Trace pages, customize your returns service, and gain valuable insights into your shipping operations through the shipments summary.

MyParcel offers flexibility in shipping options. You can either send your items with their selected carriers, taking advantage of thousands of PostNL and DHL locations for drop-off, or opt to use your own Instabox shipping contract. Additionally, MyParcel supports Verzenden via Bol orders, expanding your shipping possibilities.

Check out the [**MyParcel costs page**](https://www.myparcel.nl/en/costs/) to calculate the shipping costs based on destination and shipment type.

## How to Link Your eCommerce Store to MyParcel?

1. **Log in** to your eCommerce admin panel.
2. Navigate to ***Settings → Shipping.***
3. Scroll down and click on **"Add shipping method".**
4. On the opened modal, **select MyParcel** shipping method.
5. Paste the MyParcel API key.
6. **Enable** the the shipping method by switching the toggle and click on the **Submit** button.

Done! You have successfully connected your eCommerce store with MyParcel.

## Getting Your API Keys

To get your API keys, you need to first generate it from your MyParcel account.

To can find your API key from your [**MyParcel account**](https://account.myparcel.nl/login):

1. Head to the ***Shop settings → General***.
2. If you don't see the API key, click on the **"Generate API key"**.

## Features and Benefits

Integrating MyParcel with your eCommerce store provides several advantages:

### Streamlined Shipping Process

- **Automated Label Generation**: Create shipping labels automatically for your orders.
- **Bulk Processing**: Process multiple orders simultaneously, saving time and effort.
- **Integration with Multiple Carriers**: Access services from various carriers, including PostNL and DHL.

### Enhanced Customer Experience

- **Branded Track & Trace**: Provide your customers with a branded tracking experience.
- **Delivery Options**: Offer various delivery options, including standard delivery, evening delivery, and weekend delivery.
- **Returns Management**: Simplify the returns process for your customers.

### Operational Insights

- **Shipment Dashboard**: Get an overview of all your shipments.
- **Analytics**: Gain insights into your shipping operations.
- **Cost Management**: Easily track and manage your shipping costs.

## Shipping Options

MyParcel offers various shipping options to meet your needs:

- **Parcel Shipping**: Send packages of various sizes to destinations worldwide.
- **Letterbox Parcels**: Send smaller items that fit through a letterbox.
- **Pallet Shipping**: Transport larger items on pallets.
- **International Shipping**: Reach customers around the world with international shipping options.

## Best Practices

To make the most of your MyParcel integration, consider these best practices:

- **Accurate Product Information**: Ensure that your product weights and dimensions are accurate to provide correct shipping rates.
- **Clear Shipping Policies**: Communicate your shipping policies, including delivery times and costs, clearly to your customers.
- **Regular Updates**: Keep your MyParcel integration updated to benefit from the latest features.
- **Customer Communication**: Keep your customers informed about their shipments by providing tracking information.

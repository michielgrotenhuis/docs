# Products

This section covers everything you need to know about managing products in your eCommerce store. From creating products and setting up collections to managing inventory and handling product locations, you'll find comprehensive guidance on how to effectively showcase and organize your product catalog.

## What's in this section

- [Creating Products](creating-products) - Learn how to add and optimize product pages
- [Product Collections](product-collections) - Create and manage product collections
- [Inventory Management](inventory-management) - Track and manage your product stock levels
- [Price Rounding](price-rounding) - Understand and configure price rounding for your products
- [Locations](locations) - Manage stock and inventory locations

## Managing Your Product Catalog

Effectively managing your product catalog is essential for the success of your eCommerce store. Here's a recommended approach:

### 1. Creating Products

Start by adding products to your store with detailed information:
- Compelling product titles and descriptions
- High-quality images
- Accurate pricing
- Product variations (sizes, colors, etc.)
- Product SEO optimization

Learn more about [creating products](creating-products).

### 2. Organizing with Collections

Group your products into collections to make it easier for customers to find what they're looking for:
- Create collections based on product categories
- Set up promotional collections (featured products, new arrivals, etc.)
- Optimize collection pages for search engines

Learn more about [product collections](product-collections).

### 3. Managing Inventory

Keep track of your product stock to prevent overselling and ensure efficient order fulfillment:
- Set up initial stock quantities
- Update inventory levels as you sell products
- Configure out-of-stock behavior

Learn more about [inventory management](inventory-management).

### 4. Setting Up Locations

If you have multiple warehouses or fulfillment centers, configure your locations:
- Add all physical locations where you stock products
- Assign inventory to specific locations
- Configure fulfillment preferences

Learn more about [locations](locations).

### 5. Price Configuration

Ensure your pricing is clear and consistent:
- Set regular and sale prices
- Configure price rounding for tax-inclusive pricing
- Ensure pricing adheres to local regulations

Learn more about [price rounding](price-rounding).

By following these product management practices, you'll create a well-organized and appealing product catalog that enhances the shopping experience for your customers and drives more sales for your business.

# The Courier Guy Overview

[**The Courier Guy**](https://thecourierguy.co.za/) is a package delivery company based in South Africa. It provides courier services for businesses and individuals, specializing in the transportation of parcels and packages to various destinations within South Africa and internationally.

The Courier Guy offers a range of delivery options, including standard and express services, as well as tracking capabilities to monitor the status of shipments.

## How to Link Your eCommerce Store to The Courier Guy?

1. **Log in** to your eCommerce admin panel.
2. Navigate to ***Settings → Shipping.***
3. Scroll down and click on **"Add shipping method".**
4. On the opened modal, **select The Courier Guy** shipping method.
5. Paste the **The Courier Guy API key**.
6. **Enable** the the shipping method by switching the toggle and click on the **Submit** button.

Done! You have successfully connected your eCommerce store with The Courier Guy.

## Getting Your API Keys

To get your API keys:

1. Log in to your [**The Courier Guy account**](https://portal.thecourierguy.co.za/login).
2. Head to the ***Settings***
3. Click on the **API Keys** under the ***Account settings**.* 
4. If you don't see the API key, click on the **"Create API key"**.

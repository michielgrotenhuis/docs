## Setting Up Payment Methods

Providing convenient payment options is crucial for converting visitors into customers. Here's how to approach payment setup in your eCommerce store:

### 1. Understand Your Market

Consider your target market when selecting payment methods:
- Which payment methods are popular in your target countries?
- What currencies do your customers use?
- Are there regional payment preferences you should accommodate?

### 2. Configure Cash on Delivery (if applicable)

If you offer local delivery, you might want to enable Cash on Delivery:
- Set up payment terms and instructions
- Configure order management process for COD orders

Learn more about [Cash on Delivery](cash-on-delivery).

### 3. Set Up Online Payment Gateways

Choose and configure payment gateways based on your business needs:

#### Global Payment Solutions
- [PayPal](payment-gateways/paypal) - Widely used international payment gateway
- [Stripe](payment-gateways/stripe) - Popular payment solution with comprehensive features

#### Regional Payment Gateways
- Africa: [Paystack](payment-gateways/paystack), [Flutterwave](payment-gateways/flutterwave), [Yoco](payment-gateways/yoco)
- Latin America: [Mercado Pago](payment-gateways/mercado-pago)
- India: [Razorpay](payment-gateways/razorpay)
- Middle East: [Mamo Pay](payment-gateways/mamo-pay)
- Europe: [Nets Easy](payment-gateways/nets-easy), [Vipps](payment-gateways/vipps), [Mollie](payment-gateways/mollie)
- Turkey: [iyzico](payment-gateways/iyzico)
- Azerbaijan: [Epoint](payment-gateways/epoint), [Payriff](payment-gateways/payriff)

### 4. Test Your Payment Flow

Before launching, thoroughly test your payment configuration:
- Verify the checkout process works correctly
- Test payment gateway functionality in sandbox mode
- Ensure payment notifications and confirmations are working properly

By setting up appropriate payment methods for your target market, you'll provide a seamless checkout experience that encourages conversions and builds customer trust.

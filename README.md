# Store.icu Platform Documentation

Welcome to the official documentation for Store.icu Platform, a comprehensive eCommerce solution designed to help businesses establish and grow their online presence.

## Overview

Store.icu Platform is a unified eCommerce platform that eliminates the need for third-party apps to ensure full functionality of your online store. All essential tools needed to sell online are built-in and offered directly by Store.icu.

## Features

- **All-in-One Solution**: Get an instant online store and admin panel with all necessary tools
- **Multiple Payment Options**: Easy integration with global payment gateways including PayPal, Stripe, and region-specific providers
- **Built-in Marketing Tools**: Comprehensive range of marketing and conversion tools available in all plans
- **Mobile-First Design**: Mobile-friendly storefront and admin panel for management on the go
- **Flexible Payment Methods**: Support for various payment options including Cash on Delivery

## Getting Started

To begin using Store.icu Platform:

1. Create your account at [Store.icu](https://store.icu)
2. Follow the setup wizard to configure your store
3. Add your products and configure payment methods
4. Launch your store and start selling!

# Stock & Inventory Locations

Locations refers to a feature that allows sellers to manage the physical locations where they stock, fulfill, and sell their products. This feature typically includes the ability to add and edit location information, such as the location's name, address, and contact information, and assign different warehouse or fulfillment centers to each location for shipping.

By having accurate information about their physical locations, sellers can more easily manage their shipping and fulfillment operations. For example, they can set different shipping rates and options for different locations, or assign specific fulfillment centers to handle orders from certain areas.

By default, you'll have one location which includes the information you provide in the sign up page.

## How to Edit Locations?

You can check/edit the location from the *Settings* → *Locations* page.

To edit the location details:

- Click on the three dots ··· icon;
- Here, you can update your online store location details.

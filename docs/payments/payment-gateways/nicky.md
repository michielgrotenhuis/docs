# Activating Nicky Crypto Payment Gateway

Nicky enables crypto payments for individuals, freelancers and businesses. Payments go directly to your wallet, without Nicky touching your funds.

Under Store.icu, you can easily activate the Nicky payment gateway and use cryptocurrency for transactions.

## Setting Up the Nicky Payment Gateway in Your Store.icu Dashboard

### Step 1: Add the Payment Method

1. Log in to your eCommerce admin panel
2. Navigate to **Settings → Payments** in your admin panel
3. Click on the "**Add payment method**" button

![Add payment method screen](../static/img/nicky/add-payment-method.png)

### Step 2: Select and Configure Nicky

4. Payment gateway availability is based on store currency. If your store currency is supported by the payment gateway, then the respective payment gateway will be available for your store.
   
   If your store currency is supported, select "**Crypto Payment Through Nicky**"

![Select Nicky payment option](../static/img/nicky/select-nicky.png)

5. In the "Enable payment method" section, click on the toggle to enable the payment method
6. Enter the "**API Key**" and "**Secret Key**" from your Nicky account, then click on the Submit button

![Enter API credentials](../static/img/nicky/enter-credentials.png)

7. Once you add these two details and click submit, you can now use the Nicky payment gateway for your store transactions where your customers can make payments using cryptocurrency

## Getting Your Nicky API Keys

1. If you don't have an account with the Nicky payment gateway, you need to create one and get the API key for Nicky cryptocurrency transactions
2. Visit [nicky.me](https://nicky.me) and click on "**Sign Up**" to register yourself with this payment gateway
3. Once registered, please email [joao@nicky.me](mailto:joao@nicky.me) to get the API key for your online store
4. Add this API key under your store dashboard (using the steps mentioned above)

## Connecting Your Crypto Wallet with Nicky

Once your account is activated with Nicky.me, follow these steps to connect your crypto wallet:

1. Login to your Nicky.me dashboard
2. Click on **Settings** from top left → **Connection**
3. To connect your crypto account with your Nicky account:
   - Click on "**Add Connection**"
   - Select the appropriate option
   - Enter API secret key and name of your connection
   - Click on "**Add Connection**"

![Add connection screen](../static/img/nicky/add-connection.png)

4. Navigate to "**Routing**" (under the Settings tab)
   - Click "**Edit Route**"
   - If you've already connected your account, you'll see a list of provider-supported currencies (which are supported in your connected account)
   - Alternatively, you can connect your private wallet and enable the currencies for transactions

![Edit routing screen](../static/img/nicky/edit-routing.png)

## Completion

Once the above steps are completed, your account is ready to accept cryptocurrency transactions. Your customers can now easily make payments using cryptocurrency.

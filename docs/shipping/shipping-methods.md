# 3rd Party Shipping Providers

eCommerce supports third-party shipping method integrations.

Payment integrations can be found at:

***Settings* → *Integrations***

## Supported Shipping Providers

eCommerce integrates with various third-party shipping providers to offer flexible and efficient shipping solutions for your online store. Some of the supported providers include:

- **The Courier Guy** - A popular shipping service in South Africa
- **MyParcel** - An all-in-one shipping platform for online entrepreneurs

Each shipping provider offers different features, coverage areas, and pricing plans. By integrating with these providers, you can offer your customers a variety of shipping options and streamline your order fulfillment process.

## Benefits of Using Third-Party Shipping Providers

- **Automated Shipping Process**: Integration with third-party shipping providers can automate many aspects of the shipping process, including label generation, tracking number assignment, and status updates.

- **Cost Efficiency**: Many shipping providers offer competitive rates that can help reduce your shipping costs.

- **Expanded Shipping Options**: By integrating with multiple shipping providers, you can offer your customers a wider range of shipping options, including express shipping, international shipping, and more.

- **Improved Customer Experience**: Providing accurate shipping estimates and real-time tracking information can enhance the overall customer experience and build trust in your online store.
